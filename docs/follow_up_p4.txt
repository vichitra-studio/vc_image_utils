- vc_structure_tensor: NAME IS RIGHT, PLACEMENT IS NOT.
	- It lives in vc_harris.h, which implies it belongs to Harris. It does
	  not. The same M is Shi-Tomasi (R = min eigenvalue -- S2's eigen2x2
	  already does the readout), Lucas-Kanade/KLT (solves M*[u,v]^T = -b),
	  Foerstner, and Di Zenzo's colour tensor.
	- Do NOT rename it to anything with "harris" or "descriptor" in it.
	  "descriptor" is needed for the real thing (a vector on a detected
	  keypoint, for matching) -- see the detector/descriptor/matcher item
	  above, which is the same question from the other end.
	- MOVE IT OUT when the second caller exists, i.e. when P4's optical
	  flow needs M. Mechanical: it is a plain aggregate with no other
	  dependency on vc_harris.h. Splitting the header before that is churn.

- CHANNEL COLLAPSE IS A MISSING PIPELINE STAGE, not a missing field.
	- vc_corner has no channel and should not: a corner is a position.
	  But corner_peaks currently reads CHANNEL 0 ONLY, so on RGB it
	  silently returns corners found in red and discards G and B. That is
	  a silent wrong answer, not a deferred policy decision.
	- The reduction must happen at the TENSOR, before corner_response,
	  because R = det - k*tr^2 is NONLINEAR: sum(R(M_c)) != R(sum(M_c)).
	  A red horizontal edge crossing a green vertical edge is a corner,
	  and per-channel R says it is not.
	- sum_c M_c is J^T J for the 3x2 Jacobian, i.e. Di Zenzo 1986 -- the
	  honest generalisation, not a merge heuristic.
	- BOTH DONE. corner_peaks rejects multi-channel; corner_response
	  collapses to the mean and R is now always single-channel. What is
	  left for P4:
	    * THE COLLAPSE COULD MOVE BEFORE THE WINDOW. Summation and
	      convolution are both linear so it commutes, and collapsing
	      first means windowing 1 channel instead of C -- a 3x saving on
	      the most expensive stage, for an identical result. Not done
	      because structure_tensor() owns the window; it would need a
	      flag or a split.
	    * IT MOVES WITH vc_structure_tensor when that type leaves
	      vc_harris.h (see the item above).
	    * ITS INDEXING ASSUMES INTERLEAVED STORAGE. px[i * ch + c]
	      matches vc_image_info::index(), and the channel loop is
	      innermost BECAUSE channels are adjacent. Under the planar/
	      interleaved item above, this is one of the places that has to
	      change, and it will be silently wrong rather than loud.

- ANISOTROPY COVERAGE: CLOSED, by group 1c of 20_harris -- a reference
  comparison against a tensor built from the fused DoG kernels, matched in
  the interior to 1e-4. Kept here because the route to it is instructive.
	- Three gate designs failed first. On-axis tensor gates are blind by
	  transpose symmetry. The rotated peak count is blind at sigma_i = 2
	  (same 24 interior peaks either way) and only worked at sigma_i = 1,
	  which is the ring defect. A/B against its geometric value on a
	  tilted edge was INCONCLUSIVE, not disproved: at 45 degrees
	  transpose symmetry forces A/B = 1 for BOTH routes and the
	  diagnostic returned 1.90 and 2.03, which means the diagnostic was
	  broken (double cos45 != sin45 flips anti-diagonal pixels, and the
	  sample point sat on the step). An anti-aliased analytic edge would
	  be the way to test the idea properly, if it is ever wanted.
	- The lesson worth keeping: the working gate compares against a
	  CONSTRUCTION, not against a consequence. Consequence gates
	  (counts, ratios, peaks) all turned out to depend on the scales;
	  the construction gate does not.
	- It also EXCLUDED a construction the header used to permit: a
	  sampled analytic Gaussian derivative is 1.6e-01 away from
	  Gaussian-convolved-with-difference, further than the trap itself.
	  Header narrowed accordingly.
